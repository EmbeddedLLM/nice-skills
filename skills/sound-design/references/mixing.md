# Mix around the story

Separate speech, music, ambience, and effects. Choose the speech level first, then shape the other stems around it. Leave silence where it improves contrast. Important actions need recognizable attacks; repeated identical sounds often need less gain or deliberate variation, not more layers.

For a music-led film, establish the useful beat grid and phase from observed onsets, check half/double-tempo ambiguity, and keep event times in floating-point seconds until final frame/sample placement. Variable-tempo sections need local grids. Align a sound's meaningful attack or peak to the visual event, not automatically its file start. Confirm sync on the encoded deliverable, including mux offsets.

Measure sample peak, true peak when a suitable tool is available, integrated/short-term loudness, speech masking, channel consistency, and trailing silence on the actual output. A sample peak below zero does not establish a safe reconstructed true peak. Platform loudness targets differ; use the requested delivery spec and do not blindly force one LUFS value on every sound. Decode and listen after compression because codecs can change peaks and transients.

For external sound, inspect the source item's actual license. Free downloads from stock sites do not automatically permit redistribution as a sound library. Preserve title, author, exact source, license/version, required notice, and transformations; exclude unverified or incompatible samples. The bundled procedural bank needs no sampled audio. Automated classifiers or similarity scores cannot establish licensing or replace playback review.

If a user requests Earshot's model-based analysis, use their explicitly installed, version-pinned environment and review its model licenses and downloads separately. That analysis tier and its realism/probability claims are not part of this bundle. Paid audio generation is not required.
