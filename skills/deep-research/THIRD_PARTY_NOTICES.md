## Modifications and selected scope

### Corey Haines — makerskills

Rewritten as a portable brief and evidence plan. Removed vendor paths, external sync, fixed age penalties, and source-count confidence rules. Initial clarification is not presented as upstream support for ongoing human questioning.

### Matt Pocock — skills

Adapted dependent planning questions and parallel fact-finding to a live research brief. Removed relentless/exhaustive interrogation, leading yes-questions, approval gates, tracker mutations, pushes, and one-ticket-per-session restrictions. Continuous deep-research clarification is our adaptation.

### Collaborative Dynamics — OMNARA

Rewritten as a lightweight research loop and optional local ledger. Removed launchers, personas, campaign UI, vault implementation, paid routing, and external references. Applying its materiality rule after discoveries is our adaptation, not an empirical validation claim.

### BGGG — Reddit research

Rewritten as host-appropriate forum inspection and coverage recording. Omitted scripts, raw datasets, actual post excerpts, fixed endpoint commands, bundled web-access, and unrelated subskills. Replaced complete-thread wording with observed coverage and explicit limits.

### Matt Van Horn — last30days

Selected only source-status distinctions, observed citation URLs, dated evidence, and untrusted-source handling. Omitted runtime engine, credentials, fixtures, social excerpts, branding, presentation laws, default recent window, and engagement-based truth weighting.

### Affaan Mustafa — ECC

Rewritten as tool-neutral goal framing, full-source inspection, untrusted-source handling, claim citation and fact/inference separation. Removed mandatory vendors, fixed source quotas, forced questions, universal source-type rankings, and a blanket recent-year preference.
