STARTING A PRESENTATION
1. Install the included DejaVu fonts if they are not already available. The
   Fonts folder contains regular, bold, italic and bold-italic styles.
2. Open the appropriate PPTX to edit the example deck, or use its POTX file
   when starting a new presentation from a reusable template.
3. Save your working presentation under a new project filename.
4. Replace all bracketed fields. Sample charts, schedules and responsibility
   assignments are illustrative and must be replaced with project data.
5. Duplicate a sample slide when you need its detailed arrangement. The
   PowerPoint New Slide / Layout menus also expose the included custom layouts.
6. Read the notes pane for the short instructions that accompany each slide.
7. Keep the style guide while drafting. Hide or remove it from the presentation
   you share with your audience.

EDITING THE CONTENT
Text and shapes remain editable. Tables are native PowerPoint tables.
Charts are native PowerPoint charts with embedded example workbooks; use
Edit Data to update their categories and values. Update units and explanatory
text at the same time. Empty picture areas are intentional placeholders for
your own assets. Use images and logos you have permission to include.

The PowerPoint files use a 16:9 canvas (13 1/3 by 7 1/2 inches). Layouts,
theme colours, title/body placeholders and speaker notes are included.
Detailed sample slides can contain additional editable objects beyond the
generic New Slide layouts. Duplicating the sample is the easiest way to retain
those richer arrangements.

FONT AND APPLICATION NOTES
The fonts are supplied separately, not embedded in the editable PPTX/POTX.
Install them before editing to reproduce the intended typography. PowerPoint,
Keynote, Google Slides and LibreOffice may differ slightly in their treatment
of charts, font metrics and theme colours. The PDF is a visual reference.
