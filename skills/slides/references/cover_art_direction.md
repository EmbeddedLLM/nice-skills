# Cover design

For creating or redesigning a cover. Leave an existing cover alone unless the edit requires changing it. A cover counts toward the requested slide total, so do not add one the deck does not need.

## Starting point

Absent a supplied design that says otherwise, keep the title slide minimal and simple — do not fill it.

Begin from the user's design, the audience, and the images available. A plain title on a solid background, a source photograph, or a relevant illustration can all be right. The subject must be clear and the title readable against whatever sits behind it. Design the cover itself; a miniature webpage or a reduced slide is not a cover design.

## Layout

Match the reference's layout, including uneven spacing or images held inside generous margins when that is part of the design. Otherwise give images and text the room they need — no fixed ratio and no full-height panel is required.

Keep original logos and artwork at their own proportions. Use `cover` cropping only where it will not remove required detail; otherwise fit the whole image inside its frame.

## Text and credits

Titles and other foreground text stay editable. Source credits go in the notes unless the user, the licence, or the audience needs them on the slide.

No commentary about how the images were generated, and none about how good the design is. Generated scenes are never presented as records of real events or real objects.
