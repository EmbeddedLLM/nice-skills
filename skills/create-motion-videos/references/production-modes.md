# Additional production modes

These modes preserve useful outcomes from the former motion-video workflow using this package's deterministic time contract. They do not require the former Motion Studio engine or another skill.

| Mode | Production decisions and checks |
| --- | --- |
| Product demonstration or launch | Inspect the actual UI, brand assets, and supported claims. Show real interactions and scope revisions to accepted scenes. |
| Kinetic typography or lyric video | Keep text readable through camera motion. Align words to supplied speech/song timing and preserve punctuation and language coverage. Use only authorized recordings/lyrics. |
| Music visualizer | Derive visual events from supplied audio or a procedural score. Record cue timestamps and offsets; distinguish measured beats from estimated ones. |
| Seamless loop | Make state periodic and inspect the last-to-first transition against nearby motion steps. Omit a duplicate terminal frame; inspect audio continuity too. |
| Captions on existing footage | Preserve the original footage. Align words, test line breaks/safe areas, and inspect any masks or occlusion shot by shot. This does not authorize generating a new speaking likeness. |
| Personal or session story | Use only the history explicitly supplied or authorized for this task. Keep private text out of external services; do not collect unrelated history. |

Keep format, visual style, and motion behavior independently chosen. A score can be the master clock for a music-led film; narration takes precedence when speech carries the explanation. Stillness and intentionally stepped animation are valid design choices.

Before delivery, decode the final encode, check duration, resolution, frame count, audio presence, captions, and cue alignment. Inspect a contact sheet from the final file and playback/audio where available. Interpret freeze reports in context rather than treating every hold as a defect. Report measured checks separately from visual/listening review.

Legacy specialized templates that depended on absent photos, masks, or separate engines are not claimed as installed capabilities. Recreate a requested concept only from available, authorized assets and verify it as a new composition.
